Introduction

Welcome to EMI Reminder & Loan Tracker ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Our App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Loan Details: Loan names, EMI amounts, due dates, interest rates, and tenure
  • Payment Status: Whether an EMI has been marked as paid
  • App Preferences: Your notification settings and currency preferences
Important: All this data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT do:

  • ❌ We do NOT collect personal identification information
  • ❌ We do NOT collect financial account numbers or banking details
  • ❌ We do NOT collect location data
  • ❌ We do NOT collect usage analytics
  • ❌ We do NOT track your activity
  • ❌ We do NOT use cookies
  • ❌ We do NOT share any data with third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT require user registration or login
  • ❌ We do NOT access your contacts, camera, or other sensitive permissions

How the App Works

Offline Functionality

EMI Reminder & Loan Tracker works completely offline. No internet connection is required to use any feature of the App.

Notifications

The App uses your device's local notification system to send you EMI payment reminders. These notifications are:

  • Scheduled and triggered locally on your device
  • Not sent through any external server
  • Fully controlled by your device's notification settings

Data Storage

All loan data you enter is stored using local database on your device. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • Uninstalling the App will delete all stored data
  • We cannot access or recover your data

Permissions We Request

Permission Purpose
Notifications To send EMI payment reminders
Exact Alarm To schedule reminders at specific times
Boot Completed To restore scheduled reminders after device restart
Vibrate To vibrate device for notification alerts

We only request permissions that are essential for the App's core functionality.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's security measures (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • We recommend keeping your device secured with a screen lock
  • We do not have access to your data, so we cannot be responsible for data loss due to device damage, loss, or app uninstallation

Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. The App is a general-purpose financial reminder tool suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy within the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App
  • Modify: You can edit or update any loan information at any time
  • Delete: You can delete individual loans or clear all data by uninstalling the App
